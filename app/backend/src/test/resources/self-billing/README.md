These fixtures are the unmodified `SB-base-example.xml` and
`SB-base-creditnote-correction.xml` examples published by OpenPeppol:
https://docs.peppol.eu/poacc/self-billing/3.0/files/BIS-SelfBilling-Examples.zip

They exercise received self-billed invoices and credit notes, including validation,
customer-to-supplier addressing, and PDF rendering.
