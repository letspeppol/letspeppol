# Principles

## Who this is for

Let’s Peppol is a free, open-source way for Belgian businesses to send and receive invoices on the Peppol network.
Belgium requires structured B2B e-invoicing from 2026, so the people using this are bakers, plumbers, consultants and
their bookkeepers, not software people. They register once with their eID, then come back to send an invoice, check
what is still open, and look something up on their phone from a van or a counter.

The interface is a calm public counter: obvious, trustworthy, quick to read, nothing to learn. It is not a SaaS
dashboard and should not look like one.

## The counter, the sheet, the stamp

The logo gives us everything: a navy globe (the network, the institution) and an orange check (done, delivered).

## Deliberately not done

  <div className="ds-rule dont"><span className="ds-rule-kind">Don’t</span><div className="ds-rule-body">Gradients, glass blur or decorative radial washes on the ground.</div></div>
  <div className="ds-rule dont"><span className="ds-rule-kind">Don’t</span><div className="ds-rule-body">Cards nested inside cards.</div></div>
  <div className="ds-rule dont"><span className="ds-rule-kind">Don’t</span><div className="ds-rule-body">Numbered markers, except the registration stepper, which is a real sequence.</div></div>
  <div className="ds-rule dont"><span className="ds-rule-kind">Don’t</span><div className="ds-rule-body">Arrows appended to link or button text, middle-dot separators, all-caps eyebrows.</div></div>
  <div className="ds-rule dont"><span className="ds-rule-kind">Don’t</span><div className="ds-rule-body">Monospace for small data labels. Tabular figures in the body face do that job.</div></div>
  <div className="ds-rule dont"><span className="ds-rule-kind">Don’t</span><div className="ds-rule-body">Disabled navigation for things the person may not do. Items without permission are simply absent.</div></div>
</div>

## Where the rules live

`app/ui/DESIGN.md` is the written contract; these pages show it. Tokens and shared mechanics are defined once in
`src/app/lets-peppol.css`; page CSS sits next to each component and must not redefine shared rules.
