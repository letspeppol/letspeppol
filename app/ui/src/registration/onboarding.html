Registration checklist

  1. Register your company
  2. Prepare your eID software, reader and card
    • Install the Web eID application Windows or macOS or Linux

      Alternatively, you can install the full software stack from id.ee. Open the id.ee installation guide

    • Make sure BeIDConnect (the Belgian eID middleware) is installed. Download BeIDConnect
    • Restart your browser and check that the Web eID extension is enabled.

      Extension help for Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Microsoft Edge

    • Connect your eID reader, insert your eID card and run the Web eID check.
  3. Verify your email address.
  4. Verify your identity as a company director.
  5. Sign the contract.
  6. Sign in to your account.

Check your Web eID installation

Your card reader and browser setup look good.

If the check fails

  • Remove the eID card and insert it again.
  • Use a direct USB connection (avoid hubs/dongles).
  • Restart your browser and try again.
  • Try another computer.
  • Contact support support@letspeppol.org
